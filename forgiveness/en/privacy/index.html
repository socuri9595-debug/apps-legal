Privacy Policy

Effective September 15, 2026

Practicing Forgiveness does not collect personal information. There's no account, no sign-in and no server, and the app doesn't connect to the internet on its own. Everything you write about your hurt and your feelings stays on this device.

1. Information we collect

None. We don't receive your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or usage data. We don't use ads, analytics tools, tracking or third-party SDKs.

2. What stays on this device

Everything you write and choose in the app is stored only on this device:

· the track you chose and the name you gave the knot

· your Day progress, how hot things were running at check-in and check-out, and your measurement scores

· what you write — one-line entries, letters and replies, reflections, your line

· when you used SOS, and any trigger word you noted

These records are kept in the app's own storage, protected by iOS data protection, and left out of iCloud and computer backups. We can't see them.

App settings — your language, reminder time, which readings you've read, and the signal and interrupting move you chose on Day 6 — are kept in iOS's regular settings storage. They may be included in your device backup, but that backup stays in your Apple Account or on your computer and never comes to us.

3. Backup files you make

Settings > Your data > Export data makes a backup file encrypted with a password you choose (AES-256-GCM). The file is saved only where you choose — Files, iCloud Drive and so on — and is never sent to us. Once saved, it's kept under that service's policies.

· Your records inside the file can't be read without the password. So the app can show you what's in it before restoring, only a small label is left unencrypted: when it was made, the app version, and how many Days and entries it holds.

· If the password is lost, no one can open the file — including us.

· Restoring with Import data replaces the records on this device with the ones in the backup.

4. App lock and Face ID

App lock is off by default. When you turn it on, you set a 6-digit PIN. The PIN isn't kept as is — it's turned into a one-way hash and stored only in this device's Keychain. It doesn't sync to iCloud Keychain or move to other devices.

Face ID and Touch ID checks are done by iOS; the app only learns whether the check went through. Your face or fingerprint data never reaches the app.

5. Daily reminders

The daily reminder is off by default. When you turn it on, it arrives as a local notification scheduled on this device for the time you pick — no server is involved. Because notifications can show on the lock screen, they never include what you've written or the knot's name.

6. Payments

Payments are handled by Apple. We don't receive your card details or Apple Account information. The app only checks, on the device and through Apple's App Store, whether a purchase was made.

7. Crisis-word check

While you write in a session, the app checks — on the device only — for a few phrases that can be a sign of crisis. If it finds one, it shows you where you can talk to someone; it doesn't stop your session. Neither your writing nor the result is sent anywhere.

8. Internet, links and sharing

The app has no feature that sends anything to a server. Product information and purchase checks are handled by Apple's App Store.

· Tapping a research link opens your browser.

· Tapping Contact us opens your device's Mail app.

· Tell a friend opens the iOS share sheet with a set recommendation message — none of your records are included.

· Write a review opens Apple's rating prompt.

Those services have their own policies.

9. Children's personal information

We don't collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 14.

10. Deleting your data

We keep no copy, so deleting is in your hands.

· A single entry: delete it in My journal.

· The knot's name: change or clear it in Settings > My journey.

· All records: Settings > Your data > Delete all records.

· Everything, including app settings and app lock: Settings > Your data > Reset everything.

· Deleting the app also erases the records on this device.

Deleted records can't be brought back. Backup files you made stay wherever you saved them, so please delete those there. Your purchases stay with your Apple Account and can be unlocked again with Restore purchases.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll let you know in the app and in the web version, and update the effective date.

12. Contact

Please write to breezeholicmall@gmail.com. Email leaves your device, so you don't have to include anything from your entries.
