About

A forgiveness practice app: fourteen days, about four minutes a day, to loosen a knot you've been carrying.

What it does

Here, forgiveness isn't making up or pretending it never happened. It's practice so that what happened stops making you boil inside.

One Day at a time — breathing, a few sentences, a short line of writing. If it gets heavy, the SOS button opens a settling session anytime.

Where your writing lives

No account, no server. Everything stays on your device. You can export a password-protected backup file from Settings.

Not medical care

This is a general self-help practice tool. It doesn't replace diagnosis or treatment. If you need help right now in the US, call or text 988, or text HOME to 741741. In Korea, call 1577-0199 or 109.
