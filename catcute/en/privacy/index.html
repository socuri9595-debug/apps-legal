Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective: October 30, 2026

“Cat: Pet & Pat a Tabby” does not collect personal information. There are no accounts and no servers, and the app does not connect to the internet on its own.

1. Personal Information We Collect

None. We do not receive your name, email address, phone number, location, photos, contacts, or device identifiers.

2. What Is Stored on Your Device

The name you gave the cat, the breed you chose, the language, whether sound, haptics and notifications are on, whether you have seen the how-to, and the record of your time together — how long it slept, how long you petted it, how many butt pats and the like, along with the bond — are stored on this device. The home screen widget reads those same values from this device. Nothing leaves the device, and deleting the app deletes all of it.

3. Notifications

With “Let the cat reach out” on, the cat tells you what it is doing, at most once a day. Notifications are composed on this device and delivered by this device. No server is involved, so the developer cannot know when one appeared or what it said. You can turn it off any time in Settings, and your device asks permission the first time you turn it on.

4. Internet Connection

Lifetime product information and purchase verification are handled by Apple's App Store. Other than that, the app does not use the internet. Tapping Contact Us opens your device's mail app, and tapping a web document opens your browser.

5. Ads, Analytics, and Tracking

We do not use ads, analytics tools, tracking, or third-party SDKs.

6. Payment Information

Payments are handled by Apple. The developer does not receive your card information or Apple Account information, and the app only checks, on your device, whether Lifetime has been purchased.

7. Children's Personal Information

We do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 14.

8. Notice of Changes

If this policy changes, we will announce it in the app and in the web document and update the effective date.
