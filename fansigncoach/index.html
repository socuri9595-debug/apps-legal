K-pop Fansign Coach

Say it right. Say it in time.

Write what you want to tell your idol in any language. The app turns it into Korean a fan would really say, warns you if something could come across badly, and helps you rehearse it out loud before your video call or fansign.

What it does

Three versions of every sentence — polite, friendly and casual — with the romanisation, how it actually sounds, and what it means back in your own language. A timer keeps your script inside the seconds you are given. You can practise answering the questions idols usually ask, trace a handwritten letter, and look up fandom words other fans have added.
