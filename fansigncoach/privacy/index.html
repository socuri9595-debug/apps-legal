Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Effective 16 September 2026

Your account is anonymous. We never ask for your name, your email or a password, and we do not use ads, analytics or trackers.

What we keep

When you first open the app it creates an anonymous account — a random id, nothing that identifies you. Against that id we keep the sentences you make and save, the scripts you build, how many sentences you have made today (so the daily limit works), the language you chose, and the fact that you confirmed you are 13 or older. This is stored on Supabase servers in the United States.

What we send to write the Korean

To turn your message into Korean, the app sends the text you typed, the situation you picked, and the member name you entered (if any) to our server, which passes them to Anthropic's Claude API. Your account id is not sent with it. We do not keep a copy of your text in our logs.

Your voice

Rehearsal uses your phone's own speech recognition, and the line is read aloud by your phone's own voice. We never record your voice, and no audio is ever sent to us or stored on our servers. When your phone has the Korean recognition model installed, the listening happens entirely on the device; if it does not, your phone may send the audio to Apple or Google to turn it into text, under their own privacy policies. From all of that we keep only the text that was recognised and how closely it matched the line you practised.

The community dictionary

Entries you add to the expression dictionary are public: the expression, the meaning you wrote, your example sentence and the nickname you chose are visible to other people in the app. Do not put anything private in them. Entries are checked automatically before they appear, and anyone can report or hide them.

Payments

Subscriptions and the fansign pass are handled by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details; we only receive whether your plan is active.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account in Settings. Your sentences, scripts and rehearsal records go with it. Dictionary entries stay so the dictionary keeps working, but they lose any link to your account and your nickname is removed.

Children

This app is for people aged 13 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will say so in an app update and on this page. Contact: breezeholicmall@gmail.com
